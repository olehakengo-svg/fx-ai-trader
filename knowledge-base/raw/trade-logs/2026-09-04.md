The doc is explicit: **"これはエッジ主張ではない — API 利用料をスプレッドで払う運用コスト (見積 月 ¥1,000–4,000)"**.

**Design principle that produced the whole anomaly**: *"demo DB には一切書かない (Kelly / quant-eval / 鮮度検知の母集団を汚染しない)"* — SVK trades are identified **only** by the OANDA-side `tradeClientExtensions` tag `"SVK"` and the module's own state file. They are therefore **correctly absent** from `oanda_audit`, `by_type`, `demo_trades`, and the JPY ledger. The invisibility is the feature, and it is exactly why three consecutive daily logs could not find them.
