## What Was Built

Wave 1 Phase 0 detector and deterministic regression fixture were implemented without touching `app.py`, `modules/`, or `strategies/`.

The detector embeds the locked SQLite DDL as `SCHEMA_SQL`, calculates Wilder ATR, detects strict `k=3` swing pivots, evaluates 12 locked chart-pattern geometries, applies bar-close breakout gates, calculates Entry/SL/TP, and deduplicates pattern instances by `(pattern_id, pivot_anchor_ts, pivot_opposite_ts)`.

## Verdict

NEEDS_MORE_EVIDENCE.

The detector implementation and locked synthetic regression layer are complete, but W1P0 cannot be accepted until the real USDJPY M5 parquet is present and `tools/s6_run_w1p0.py` produces:
- total signals >=360,
- 12 pattern rows in SQLite aggregation,
- duplicate pivot tuple query returns 0 rows,
- duration median/p95 and pattern-height ATR distributions.

## Residual Risks

- Real-market hit counts may be sparse for one or more locked geometries, especially flags and H&S variants.
- The fixture is deterministic synthetic regression, not human visual annotation; true manual precision/recall remains Wave 1 Phase 1 work.
- Production runtime on 903,828 bars was not measured in this workspace.
